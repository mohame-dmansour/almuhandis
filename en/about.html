Company Overview

Al-Muhandis General Contracting Company is one of the leading companies in the contracting sector in Egypt. The company was founded in 2012 by Engineer Hassan Mandour, where it officially began operations in the Egyptian market.

The company has executed a large number of projects in the petroleum sector, industrial sectors, governmental sector, residential sector, and commercial sector.

We are committed to providing the highest standards of quality and professionalism in every project, while ensuring client satisfaction and building long-term relationships based on trust and transparency.
